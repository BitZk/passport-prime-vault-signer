# Code of conduct

Contributors are expected to be respectful, precise, and security-conscious.

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Assume good intent while requiring evidence for security claims.
- Do not pressure anyone to publish private contact information, wallet data, device data, or vulnerability details.
- Respect coordinated disclosure and maintainer requests to move sensitive discussion to a private channel.
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and deliberate secret exposure are not tolerated.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or users.
